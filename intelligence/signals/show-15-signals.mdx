## Market Signals

- **Cultural Bottlenecks**: Identification of human and organizational culture as the primary limiting factor in enterprise AI implementation, overstepping technical constraints.
- **Strategic Readiness Workshops**: The emergence of specialized AI implementation consulting for regional mid-size and enterprise clients.
- **Regional Safe Spaces**: The need for localized, low-friction environments for founders and stakeholders to discuss the conflicted "promise and peril" of AI.
- **Curation over Slop**: High-value regional intelligence emerges as the primary defense against the proliferation of low-effort AI content in the media landscape.
