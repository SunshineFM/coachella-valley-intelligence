## Market Signals

- **Agentic Workflow Standardization**: The release of Anthropic’s CoWork agent as a leading indicator of the shift toward natural language workflow automation for non-technical users.
- **Personal Intelligence Privacy**: A growing tension between the utility of "God-mode" personal assistants and the data privacy concerns of individual users.
- **Platform Monetization**: The introduction of ads within ChatGPT signaling the transition of foundational AI models into traditional ad-supported media ecosystems.
- **Regional Workforce Career Ladders**: The necessity of localized AI training communities to ensure the next generation of desert workers can compete in a automated labor market.
