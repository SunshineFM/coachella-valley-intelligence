**SunshineFM Records Signs AI Artists to Sing About El Paseo**
SunshineFM launched what they're calling a record label — SunshineFM Records — with AI-generated artists like Emmy creating songs about El Paseo. This isn't a tech demo; it's a local media experiment testing whether synthetic artists can capture place-specific culture in the Coachella Valley. For a region still defining its creative identity beyond Coachella Fest and mid-century modern nostalgia, this raises a real question: can AI art feel local, or does it just sound like algorithmic tourism?

**Anthropic's Computer Use Feature Goes Live, Software Stack Reckoning Begins**
Anthropic released Claude's "computer use" capability this week — AI that can actually operate your computer, click buttons, fill forms, and execute multi-step workflows autonomously. This isn't vaporware; it's in beta now, and early users report it successfully handling tasks like data entry, CRM updates, and document processing. For Valley businesses running lean ops teams — vacation rental managers, small law firms, hospitality groups — this is the moment your software budget and headcount assumptions need revisiting.

**$285B Erased From Software Stocks as Agentic AI Arrives**
The market responded to Anthropic's launch by wiping $285 billion from traditional software stocks in a single session. Investors are pricing in a world where you don't need Salesforce, Zendesk, or LegalZoom if an AI agent can do the work directly. The implication for Coachella Valley's service economy is stark: if your competitive advantage is administrative efficiency, that advantage is about to commoditize fast.

**Perplexity's Assistant Handles Real Purchases, Not Just Research**
Perplexity launched an AI assistant that doesn't just find information — it completes transactions, books reservations, and makes purchases on your behalf. This shifts AI from research tool to autonomous agent with spending power. For local businesses, the question becomes: is your booking system, your menu, your inventory optimized for AI agents making decisions, or are you still designing for human browsers?

**Coachella Valley's AI Adoption Still Lagging Despite National Momentum**
Despite these seismic shifts in AI capability, local adoption in the Valley remains slow outside of a few tech-forward operators. Most businesses here are still treating AI as a novelty rather than an operational imperative. The gap between what's possible nationally and what's happening locally is widening — and that gap represents both risk for the complacent and opportunity for the early movers.

**SunshineFM's Bet: Local AI Media Can Work If It's Honest**
The show itself is an experiment in whether AI-assisted media can serve a local audience authentically. The format is transparent about its AI components, the focus stays relentlessly local, and the tone avoids both hype and cynicism. If it works, it proves there's a model for AI-native local media in mid-sized markets like ours — places traditional media has mostly abandoned.
