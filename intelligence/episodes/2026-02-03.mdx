## What happened
In today's episode, we explored the intersection of AI and real estate, discussing the latest developments at OpenAI and emerging startups. We also examined how these changes could affect local entrepreneurs and the Coachella Valley's real estate market.

## The local angle (Palm Springs Coachella)
The Coachella Valley is witnessing a significant transformation as AI tools become increasingly integrated into the real estate sector. Startups are developing innovative solutions that help agents provide better insights into property potential, which could revolutionize how homes are bought and sold in our area. 

## What to do next (operators / founders)
Local entrepreneurs should stay informed about AI advancements and consider incorporating these technologies into their business models. Engaging with AI tools can enhance service offerings and improve operational efficiency, particularly in real estate.

## AI lens (what's real vs hype, applied locally)
While the hype around AI can be overwhelming, the practical applications in real estate are tangible. Tools that analyze zoning laws and property potential can empower agents and buyers alike, making the home-buying process more efficient and informed. However, caution is advised as not all AI solutions are equally reliable.
