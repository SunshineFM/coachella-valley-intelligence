# AI, Real Estate, and the Future of Palm Springs

## What happened
In this episode, we explored the intersection of AI and local business, particularly in real estate. We discussed the recent shifts at OpenAI, the emergence of new AI startups, and the implications for entrepreneurs in the Coachella Valley. Notably, we highlighted a new AI tool designed for real estate agents that can instantly assess zoning laws and property potential, marking a significant shift in how real estate transactions may occur.

## The local angle (Palm Springs Coachella)
The Coachella Valley is witnessing a transformation in real estate practices thanks to AI. A new startup is developing an AI operating system specifically for real estate agents, allowing them to provide clients with immediate insights into property potential and zoning laws. This could revolutionize how homes are bought and sold in Palm Springs, enhancing the role of agents from mere sellers to investment advisors.

## What to do next (operators / founders)
Local entrepreneurs and real estate professionals should consider integrating AI tools into their practices. Staying informed about new technologies and participating in workshops can help them remain competitive. Engaging with AI solutions like the new operating system for agents could provide a unique selling proposition in a crowded market.

## AI lens (where it's real vs overhyped)
While AI continues to be a buzzword, its practical applications are becoming more evident in real estate and other sectors. The excitement around tools like Codex and Claude Code indicates a genuine shift towards automation and efficiency. However, caution is warranted, especially with emerging platforms that may not yet have proven reliability. The hype surrounding AI must be balanced with a critical understanding of its limitations and potential risks.
