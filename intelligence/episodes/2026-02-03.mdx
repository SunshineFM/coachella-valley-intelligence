## What happened
In today's episode, we delved into the evolving landscape of AI, particularly focusing on its implications for local industries in Palm Springs and the Coachella Valley. We discussed the recent shifts at OpenAI, the rise of Anthropic, and the potential of AI in real estate and hospitality.

## The local angle (Palm Springs Coachella)
The Coachella Valley is witnessing a unique intersection of technology and local business. With AI tools becoming more prevalent, real estate agents can now leverage AI to provide clients with insights on property potential, zoning laws, and more. This could transform how properties are bought and sold in the region, making agents more like investment advisors.

## What to do next (operators / founders)
- **Real Estate Agents**: Embrace AI tools that can enhance your service offerings and provide clients with more comprehensive insights.
- **Hospitality Operators**: Consider how AI can streamline operations and improve customer service, while also maintaining the human touch that guests value.
- **Tech Founders**: Stay informed about the latest AI developments and think about how they can be applied to local industries.

## AI lens (how AI applies, where it's overhyped, where it's real)
- **Real Applications**: AI is making significant strides in real estate, helping agents assess property potential and streamline processes.
- **Overhyped**: The idea that AI will completely replace human roles in hospitality is exaggerated; the human element remains crucial.
- **Where It's Real**: AI's ability to analyze vast amounts of data quickly and accurately is a game changer for many industries, including real estate and hospitality.
