## What happened
In this episode aired on February 4, 2026, Sat discusses the implications of Anthropic's recent AI advancements, particularly their Claude Cowork tool, which is shaking up traditional software markets and prompting a stock market sell-off. The episode emphasizes the need for local businesses in the Coachella Valley to adapt to these changes and explore AI's potential benefits.

## The local angle (Palm Springs Coachella)
The Coachella Valley, with its high concentration of real estate and legal firms, stands at a crossroads due to the introduction of AI tools like Claude Cowork. These tools promise to automate administrative tasks, potentially increasing efficiency but also threatening the traditional hourly billing model of many local firms. Sat encourages local entrepreneurs to experiment with AI technologies to remain competitive and reduce overhead costs.

## What to do next (operators / founders)
Local business owners are urged to:
- Experiment with AI tools to streamline operations.
- Engage with the AICV community for support and resources.
- Stay informed about AI developments to leverage new technologies effectively.

## AI lens (where it's real vs overhyped)
While AI's potential to automate tasks is real, Sat cautions against rushing to replace existing systems without thorough experimentation. The fear of job losses due to AI is prevalent, but there's also the promise of creating new efficiencies and opportunities for growth in the Coachella Valley.
