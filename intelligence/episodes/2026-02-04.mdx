## What happened
In this episode of Sunshine FM, Sat delves into the latest developments in AI and their implications for local businesses in Palm Springs Coachella. He discusses the recent stock market tumble linked to Anthropic's new AI capabilities, which could disrupt traditional software services, particularly in legal and real estate sectors. Sat emphasizes the importance of community involvement in adapting to these changes and invites listeners to share their experiences with AI.

## The local angle (Palm Springs Coachella)
The Coachella Valley is witnessing significant shifts due to advancements in AI technology. With a high concentration of legal and real estate transactions, local businesses face both challenges and opportunities. Sat highlights how tools like Anthropic's Claude Cowork can automate routine tasks, potentially allowing firms to manage more work with fewer resources, while also raising concerns about job security in these sectors.
