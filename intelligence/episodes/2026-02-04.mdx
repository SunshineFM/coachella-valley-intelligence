## AI lens (how AI applies, where it's overhyped, where it's real)
- **Real:** AI tools are revolutionizing traditional business operations, particularly in automating routine tasks.
- **Overhyped:** The notion that AI will completely replace jobs without creating new roles is misleading; AI often serves as a supportive tool.
- **Potential:** There's significant promise in using AI for creative projects, as demonstrated by local artists and musicians.
