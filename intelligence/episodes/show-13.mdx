## The IO Orchestration Agent

I’m introducing IO, our orchestration agent. In the engineering world, I/O is about the flow of information, and that’s exactly what this agent does for Sunshine FM. It helps us surface the vital topics from the last 24 hours and provides a mechanical, logical perspective that balances my own human intuition. We’re testing the boundaries of collaboration here. Can a single human launch and manage a radio station? Traditionally, you’d need a floor full of people for sales, operations, and on-air personality. But with agents like IO, we’re seeing if we can compress that entire infrastructure into a highly efficient, AI-augmented stack.
