## Anthropic's CoWork: Successes and Fails

I’ve been spending the last few days playing with Anthropic's non-technical coding agent, CoWork. It’s brilliant in concept—setting the standard for how non-coders can build complex workflows—but I’ve had my share of successes and absolute fails. It’s a reminder that we’re still in the experimental phase. We’re also watching ads starting to creep into ChatGPT, which signals the shift from 'pure tool' to 'ad-supported platform.' We have to be mindful of who owns the intelligence we’re building on.
