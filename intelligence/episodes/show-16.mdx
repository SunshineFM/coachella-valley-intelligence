Hey, This is Sat. We’re leaning into the 'retro digital' vibe today, because even as we head into a world of everything-AI, that human connection feels more vital than ever.

## The Analog Yearning

I’ve been having a lot of conversations in town and with friends in Silicon Valley, and there’s this palpable yearning for the 'glory days'—what I call the analog era. My argument isn't that humans will go away; quite the opposite. Sunshine FM is a safe space for those who feel the tension between the promise of the future and the comfort of the past. We’re experimenting with AI and humans collaborating to create something from scratch, but we’re doing it with a desert signature that honors our retro roots.

## PS Getaway: The London-to-Desert Signal

I’m playing a lot of Ronnie today. She’s an artist out of London who’s putting together an album about her 'PS Getaway.' It’s the perfect sonic backdrop for what we’re building here—global talent synthesizing the desert experience through AI tools. Whether it’s driving a rented Tesla out from Los Angeles or checking into a suite to escape the London rain, it’s about making a choice to find space in a world that’s moving too fast. We’re the ghost in the Gucci slide, taking the ego for a little ride into the future. Talk tomorrow.
