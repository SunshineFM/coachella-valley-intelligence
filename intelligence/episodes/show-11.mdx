Hey, This is Sat. We’re placing a marker in the sand today with an experiment that’s going to run all year long, and honestly, it’s mostly about seeing if one human and an army of agents can actually pull this off.

## The One Human, Many Agents Experiment

I opened the show today talking about my love for radio—from San Clemente to Claremont—and why Sunshine FM is finally possible. The era of AI has reached a point where it actually feels plausible for a single founder, armed with the right tools, to build a modern media brand in public. When I say 'we,' I’m talking about me and a literal army of AI agents: co-hosts, research assistants, producers, and even a station manager. We’re building this to track how AI reshapes work and life in Palm Springs Coachella, and we’re doing it without the polish of a professional broadcast. It’s an open experiment, it’s going to be weird, and it starts right now.

## Curiosity as a Strategy

The goal here isn't perfection; it’s curiosity. We’re going to be playing with AI-generated music, jingles, and voices to see what’s possible. If you’re listening, you’re already part of the experiment. We’re going to spend the year figuring out where these tools take all of us. No matter if it’s business, culture, or just a new sound, we’re surfacing the signal from the noise for our region. Welcome to the first day of the rest of our AI lives. Talk tomorrow.
