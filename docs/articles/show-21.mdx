Hey, This is Sat. We’re talking about the CEO of Anthropic today, and why his recent comments at Davos should have every founder in the Valley leaning in.

## The Case for AI Breakers

Dario Amodei, the voice behind Claude, just gave a series of interviews stating that governments need to act *now*—not in five years—to set up guardrails for the foundational models we’re building. He’s essentially talking about 'breakers' for a technology that even the architects don't fully control yet. It’s a powerful signal when a leader of a major AI company calls for more regulation. It gives us the lane to talk about the perils of AI alongside the promise, especially as these tools begin to impact our regional labor markets.
