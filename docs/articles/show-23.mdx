## Stop Looking for a Guru

One of the biggest mistakes I see enterprises making right now is putting all their AI hopes on one person. They look for the "AI Guru" or the "AI Ambassador" and think that one hire is going to solve their transformation problems. It won't. In fact, it’s a recipe for failure. If you are running an organization, you need an AI Council. You need a small group that includes people from HR, Legal, Tech, Sales, and Marketing. Each one of these departments has a completely different relationship with the technology, and you need that diversity of perspective to understand where the tools actually fit. You need the legal team to worry about data privacy while the marketing team looks for efficiency gains. Don't chase every shiny new model that drops on Twitter on a Tuesday afternoon. Instead, build a robust process of experimentation. Put together a council that can test these tools in the real world and see which ones provide actual resilience and adaptability. The goal isn't to pick the "best" model today; it’s to build a structure that can handle whatever model comes out tomorrow.
