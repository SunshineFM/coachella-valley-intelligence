Hey, This is Sat. We’re moving into a week where the industry is calling it 'vibe coding,' and it’s essentially democratizing software creation for anyone who can describe a problem.

## CES and the AI Body

CES just gave us a look at the 'AI body'—from robots to smart devices—but the real story is the 'agentic brain' behind it all. We’re reaching a point where AI isn't just something we talk to; it’s something that does things for us. Whether it’s a tool that builds your startup's website while you sleep or a system that manages your schedule, the role of the professional developer is shifting from writing code to orchestrating an army of agents. That’s exactly what we’re doing here at Sunshine FM, and 2026 is the year this becomes the baseline for how business operates.

## Overcoming the Context Barrier

One of the biggest hurdles we’ve faced for four years is the context window—the 'memory' limit of AI models. But with the rise of agentic autonomy and better checkpointing, we’re finally starting to overcome those limits. It means AI can handle more complex, longer-term tasks without losing the thread. For our local businesses, this means the 'vibe' can finally scale without losing the intelligence. If you missed the latest Hard Fork podcast, they dive deep into this shift, and I highly recommend it for anyone trying to understand the technical interface of our new reality. Talk tomorrow.
