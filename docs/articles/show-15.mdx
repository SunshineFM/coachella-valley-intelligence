## Culture vs. Technology

Over the last 18 months of running workshops for mid-size and enterprise clients, I’ve learned one vital lesson: the bottleneck for AI implementation is almost always cultural, not technical. You can spend all the money you want on agentic workflows and premium tools, but if your leadership and your workforce aren't on the same page, you’re just going to stumble. It’s about the people and the organizational readiness. If you’re trying to build an AI-native company in the desert, you have to solve for the culture before you can solve for the code.
