## Amazon: The Guardrails of Modern Medicine

Amazon is finally plugging AI into its One Medical healthcare service, but they’re doing it with a level of caution that feels almost retro. Instead of letting a model diagnose you in a vacuum, they’re positioning these AI agents as high-output administrative assistants for doctors. It’s a brilliant template for how we’re going to see AI integrated into high-stakes, regulated industries here in the Valley. It’s not about replacing the expert; it’s about the fact that even the best doctor only has two hands. If we can give them the equivalent of a dozen digital interns to handle the 'data sludge,' they might actually have time to look you in the eye during a consultation.

## Phoebe: The Agentic Future of Home Care

Phoebe is the startup that every business owner and family in the desert needs to be watching right now, but not for the reasons you might think. While the subtext is about what Agentic AI can do, the main driver here is the home care market. If you look at the home care business in Palm Springs Coachella, it’s a massive employer, but the logistics are a nightmare—scheduling, matching caregivers with patients, insurance documentation, and regulatory compliance. Phoebe is deploying AI agents to handle that backend weight, allowing the human caregivers to actually focus on the care. This isn't just a tech story; it's a home care story. We’re seeing AI move from being a 'conversation' to being the 'plumbing' that keeps our most vital human services running.
