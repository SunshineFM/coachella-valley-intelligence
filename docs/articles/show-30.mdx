## The Record Label on El Paseo

We’re officially calling it a record label: Sunshine FM Records. Today, we featured two of our AI artists, Emmy and Rani, both tackling the El Paseo vibe. Emmy’s track hits that Stone Eagle lifestyle—Stone Eagle being that high-end club up in Palm Desert—while Rani’s 'Rich Girls Avenue' captures the scene on the street. It’s an experiment in localizing the sound of the Valley. We isn't just playing music; we’re creating the soundtrack for the regional economy using tools that didn't exist two years ago.

## The Attention Bottleneck

The real problem I’m trying to solve this year is the attention bottleneck. There is more information about AI, tech, and business than any human can process. Reddit, newsletters, podcasts—it’s endless. Most of it is noise because it’s difficult to make sense of the talking heads. We’re seeing a slight slowdown in the news cycle this week—a 'catch your breath' moment—and that’s actually a good thing for the 'normies' in the real world. It gives us space to focus on implementation rather than just reacting to the next breakthrough.

## The Desert Protest Song

We’re experimenting with some more aggressive AI formats today—specifically, a 'diss track' and a 'protest song.' One is about the local outcry in Palm Springs over the high-rise buildings and the $126 million convention center deal. It’s based on the real-world friction between development and the citizens of Palm Springs. The other is my take on a protest song about what's happening more broadly. Sunshine FM is your AI signal for the Coachella Valley, and sometimes that signal needs to be loud.
