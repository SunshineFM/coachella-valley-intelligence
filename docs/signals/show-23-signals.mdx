# Show 23 Signals: The Intelligence Economy

Hey, This is Sat. Show 23 was about the big shift from tool-chasing to the Intelligence Economy.

## Business Opportunity: Intelligence Orchestration Agency
A pivot for traditional marketing or IT firms in the valley. Stop selling "tools" and start selling "Orchestration." Managing how a business’s agents interact to create a cohesive outcome is the most valuable role in 2026.

## Business Opportunity: The AI Council Implementation
Helping enterprises set up and manage an internal AI Council. This avoids the "AI Guru" single-point-of-failure and ensures the HR, Legal, and Tech departments are all working toward the same regional goal.

## Business Opportunity: Startup Gravity as a Service
Selling the desert to the Bay Area builders. Creating the business infrastructure and community ties that make an eighteen-month relocation feel seamless for a newly minted tech founder. This is economic development reimagined.
