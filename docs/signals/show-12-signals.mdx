# Show 12 Signals: Vibe Coding and Orchestration

Hey, This is Sat. Show 12 was all about the move from coding to orchestration. Here are the signals for the builders.

## Business Opportunity: Vibe Coding for Non-Technical Founders
The democratization of software creation means non-coders can now build their own tools. Founders can create a business around "Vibe Coding Workshops," helping local entrepreneurs turn their ideas into functional software without a CS degree.

## Business Opportunity: Agent Manager Services
As professional programming shifts toward orchestration, there is a massive opportunity to provide "Agent Management" as a service for local small businesses. Helping an Indio shop owner manage five agents is more valuable than writing one custom app.

## Business Opportunity: Generative Web Development
The 2026 reality is a tool that builds a startup’s website while they sleep. Small agencies in the valley should pivot from "doing" the work to "managing the agents" who do the work, vastly increasing their scale and speed.
