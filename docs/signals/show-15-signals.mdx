Most companies think they need tools, but they actually need an audit of their internal culture. Providing "AI Culture Audits" for mid-size Coachella Valley firms—analyzing how departments interact with data—is a high-value entry point.

The cost of high-quality, relevant ad jingles just hit zero. A small boutique shop could generate hundreds of shelf-ready jingles for Indio and Palm Springs businesses, charging for the curation and placement rather than the production.
