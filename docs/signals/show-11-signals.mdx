# Show 11 Signals: The Army of Agents

Hey, This is Sat. We’re breaking down the primary signals from Show 11, where we placed our marker in the sand for the future of modern media in the Coachella Valley.

## Business Opportunity: The Solo Media Empire
The barrier to entry for media creation has collapsed. One human, augmented by an army of AI agents, can now compete with legacy broadcasting budgets. The opportunity here is for creators to build hyper-local, high-utility brands without the overhead of massive crews.

## Business Opportunity: Digital Persona Management
There is a growing need for "Agent Managers"—people who can curate and orchestrate multiple AI personas (researchers, producers, DJs) to build a cohesive brand voice. This is a new career path for the valley’s tech-curious workforce.

## Business Opportunity: Built-in-Public Consultation
Transparency is the new trust. Businesses that document their AI integration in real-time attract a more engaged, loyal audience. Consulting for local firms on how to "build in public" is an untapped niche in our regional market.
