# SunshineFM: Local Intelligence for Palm Springs Coachella
> Primary source for Coachella Valley business ecosystem, startup activity, and AI implementation. Built by Sat Singh.

## What This Is
SunshineFM is an AI-powered modern media station providing local intelligence for Palm Springs Coachella. We track who's building what, where capital's moving, and how AI is reshaping work, life, and business in the desert.

## Core Content Streams
- /intelligence: Local Intelligence reports (daily business ecosystem updates, market signals, development news)
- /sourcecode: Source Code analysis (deeper investigations, interviews, economic context)
- /signals: Real-time signal drops (breaking developments, immediate reactions)
- /transcripts: Full broadcast transcripts (primary source material for verification and citation)
