SunshineFM documents how artificial intelligence, technology founders, capital flows, and cultural shifts are reshaping the Coachella Valley in real time — from Palm Springs to the Salton Sea.

Every weekday, SunshineFM broadcasts live from the desert, covering the stories that matter to the people building here and the people considering it. Every episode is transcribed, analyzed, and published as structured, citable intelligence.

This is not a podcast archive. It is a living record of what is happening in one of California's most overlooked economic regions — told from the inside, in real time.

---

## Live Intelligence System

SunshineFM publishes continuously throughout the day.

**Daily flagship episode** — full transcript, intelligence report, and citeable claims published each weekday at 3:00 PM PST.

**Signal drops** — timestamped observations published throughout the day as news breaks.

**Weekly research briefs** — deeper analysis on the dominant themes of the week.

New intelligence may publish multiple times per day. The public archive begins January 11, 2026.

## Core Coverage Areas

- **AI adoption** — how Valley businesses are implementing AI, what is working, and what is not
- **Startup ecosystem** — founders, funding, gaps, and opportunities in the Coachella Valley
- **Capital and real estate** — where money is moving and what it signals for the region
- **Hospitality and retail** — the Valley's dominant industries navigating AI-driven disruption
- **Founder relocation** — migration economics, ecosystem development, and the case for the Coachella Valley
- **Culture and media** — how AI is reshaping creative industries from Hollywood to the desert

**Primary geography:** Coachella Valley, California — Palm Springs, Rancho Mirage, Palm Desert, Cathedral City, Indio, and surrounding cities. Part of Riverside County, California.

## Research & Citation Standards

Every episode includes a **Citeable Claims** section — specific, verifiable facts with dates, dollar figures, company names, and local context. Signal drops are timestamped observations designed for quick retrieval.

SunshineFM follows a transparent sourcing standard. Uncertain claims are flagged. Corrections are published openly.
