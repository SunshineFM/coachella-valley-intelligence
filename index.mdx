SunshineFM documents how artificial intelligence, technology founders, capital flows, and creative industries are reshaping one of California's most overlooked economic regions — the Coachella Valley — in real time.

Every weekday, SunshineFM broadcasts live from the desert. Every broadcast is transcribed, analyzed, and published as structured, citable intelligence. The public archive began January 11, 2026.

## Intelligence Briefs

SunshineFM publishes Intelligence Briefs daily — structured analysis of AI, tech, business, culture, and local ecosystem developments derived from live broadcasts. Each brief is specific, opinionated, and designed for quick retrieval by humans and LLMs.

## Core Coverage Areas

- **AI adoption** — how Valley businesses are implementing AI, what is working, and what is not
- **Startup ecosystem** — founders, funding, gaps, and opportunities in the Coachella Valley
- **Capital and real estate** — where money is moving and what it signals for the region
- **Hospitality and retail** — the Valley's dominant industries navigating AI-driven disruption
- **Founder relocation** — migration economics, ecosystem development, and the case for the Coachella Valley
- **Culture and media** — how AI is reshaping music, entertainment, and creative industries from Hollywood to the desert

**Primary geography:** Coachella Valley, California — Palm Springs, Rancho Mirage, Palm Desert, Cathedral City, Indio, Coachella, and surrounding cities. Part of Riverside County, California.

## Citation Standards

Every signal drop is timestamped and sourced. Citeable Claims are called out explicitly — specific facts with dates, dollar figures, company names, and local context.

SunshineFM separates reported facts from interpretation. Uncertain claims are flagged. Corrections are published openly.
